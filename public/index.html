Suggested Order

For a new term, it's easiest to work through the pages in this order:
1. Faculty — add instructors (or bulk-import them)
2. Subjects — add subjects with units and hours
3. Rooms — add lecture rooms and labs with capacity
4. Sections — create sections and attach subjects to each
5. Assign Instructors — assign a faculty member to every section/subject pair
6. Generate Schedule — auto-generate, review, and adjust
